## The guided setup

On first start Dynamic offers a guided setup («Configurazione guidata»). It walks you through the main choices: the AI provider, the microphone and voice, and the island's look. Every step can be skipped and changed later.

You can reopen it at any time from **Settings → Generale → Riapri la configurazione guidata**.

## What works without any setup

- Local commands in the Chat: timers, reminders, notes, music, volume and navigation.
- Notes, Time (timers, alarms, reminders) and the calendar month view.
- Music controls for the Windows media session that is playing.
- The System page with hardware data.

## Suggested next steps

1. **AI**: connect ChatGPT or Gemini in **Settings → AI e Chat** (see [AI integrations](../ai/)).
2. **Voice**: pick your microphone and download Parakeet Ultra in **Settings → Voce** (see [Voice assistant](../voice/)).
3. **Look**: choose a theme and a compact style in **Settings → Aspetto e Home** (see [The island](../island/)).
4. **Start with Windows**: turn it on in **Settings → Generale → Avvio con Windows** if you want Dynamic always available.

> Choose English or Italian in the setup wizard or in **Settings → General → Language**. Some screenshots show the Italian interface.

The **Panoramica** (Overview) page in Settings shows what is still missing, such as an AI provider that is not connected.
