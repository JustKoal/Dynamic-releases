## Open and close

- Click the island, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> (global by default) to show or hide it.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> reduces the open panel.
- The icon bar at the bottom holds up to nine sections plus **Altro** (More), search and the microphone.

## Compact style and theme

In **Settings → Aspetto e Home** (Appearance and Home):

- **Forma dell'isola**: Premium, Capsule, Indicator or Adaptive, which changes width with the activities in progress.
- **Temi**: twelve themes, namely Dynamic Original, Blue, Violet, Green, Red, Midnight, Ocean, Aurora, Sunset, Emerald, Graphite and Glass. Glass is translucent when Windows **Transparency effects** are on; with them off it uses an opaque grey.
- **Colori** and **Sfondo**: accent colour, eye colour and background.
- **Personaggio dell'isola**, **Sguardo** and **Animazioni degli occhi**: the character, whether the eyes follow the cursor and how expressive they are.
- **Barra di navigazione**: pin, reorder or remove sections in the icon bar.

## Position and visibility

In **Settings → Generale**:

- **Visibilità dell'isola**: always visible or automatic hiding.
- **Monitor e posizione**: choose the monitor, centre or re-dock the island.
- **Schermo intero e giochi**: hide while a fullscreen app is on the same monitor, plus the Gaming-mode options (see [Gaming mode](../gaming-mode/)).

## Notifications

Events from messaging, agents, timers and Windows appear directly in the compact island. Duration, quiet mode and which Windows events to show are in **Settings → Notifiche**.
